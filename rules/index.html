Server Rules

Effective 4 December 2025

1. Do not be toxic, and treat other players with respect.

  • Do not use slurs or other offensive language, post shock content, or do anything else to intentionally upset other players.

  • Do not attempt to slander other players behind their backs or make vague accusations against them. If you have an issue with another player on the server, please inform a staff member.

  • Dysania is not the place for real world drama. Please keep discussions civil and keep personal issues with other players out of the server. Again, if you have an issue with another player or believe they may be breaking the rules, please do not hesitate to reach out to a staff member either on the server or in DMs for assistance.

  • Discussions of sensitive real-world topics (e.g. politics and religion) fall under this rule. If discussions get to heated, staff reserves the right to stop the discussion or mute involved users who are contributing to the toxic environment.

2. Do not make use of game modifications that may provide an unfair advantage over other players

  • This includes “hacked clients” that make use of game exploits to provide combat hacks, movement hacks, etc., bots that perform tasks in the game without player interaction (e.g. Baritone), combat clients that provide features helpful for combat that aren’t in the base game (e.g. Badlion and Lunar), and resource packs that provide an unfair advantage over other players in various ways (e.g. x-ray).

  • Specific disallowed features include combat hacks, movement hacks, x-ray functionality, and game automation (e.g. Baritone).

  • Please note that this list is not exhaustive, and whether a game modification is or isn’t allowed to be used on the server is solely up to staff discretion.

  • Note that performance enhancing mods are allowed, provided they are not bundled with other disallowed functionality. A list of preapproved game modifications, in addition to a list of explicitly banned mods, can be found below.

  • If you are unsure of whether a game modification is allowed on the server, please do not hesitate to reach out to a staff member for assistance.

3. Do not make use of alt accounts on the server.

  • Alt accounts will be banned immediately, and a violation of this rule may lead to your main account being banned as well.

  • This rule applies to both the Discord as well as the MC server itself.

4. Respect the privacy of other players.

  • Do not record audio or video content from voice calls without the consent of everyone within the call. This includes calls that take place in DMs or in any other DysaniaMC adjacent space. “Clipping” other people’s voices via Discord’s clipping feature, NVIDIA Shadowplay, or any other similar replay software is included under this rule.

  • Minecraft logs are considered to be outside the scope of RP and cannot be demanded by other players for in-game purposes. These logs can contain sensitive content as well as private chat messages and content from other servers that is not appropriate to demand for any purpose.

5. In-game and real-world matters should be kept separate.

  • Do not bring real-world drama, biases, or politics into in-game matters, and do not turn in-game matters into real-world disputes.

6. Keep griefing to a minimum.

  • Do not grief the property of other players without a valid political and/or practical reason.

  • You are NOT allowed to indiscriminately destroy the property of anybody else on the server, even if you are at war with them. Wartime damage should be kept to a minimum, and should be limited to valid wartime targets, such as storage areas, farms producing war supplies, and bunkers. Note that these areas may also be valid targets during peacetime, and griefing rules during wartime and during peacetime do not differ.

  • This rule also applies to mobs and other (non-player) entities as well.

  • Any damage caused by griefing considered to be excessive may be rolled back by staff. Staff has final discretion on what constitutes excessive damage.

7. Do not abuse glitches within the game.

  • For the purpose of this rule, a “glitch” is defined as an unintentional game feature that is capable of giving a player an advantage that they otherwise would not have.

  • Examples of glitches include duping mechanisms, lag machines, etc.

  • Do not attempt to intentionally lag or crash the server through the construction of lag machines, entity spam, or by any other means. Staff may remove, without compensation, structures built for this purpose.

  • TNT duping is allowed for use in autofarms (e.g. automated cobblestone farm). TNT dupers may not be used outside of this context, especially during wartime. World eaters are not permitted under any circumstance.

  • If you are unsure of whether something in the game constitutes a glitch, please feel free to ask an admin.
